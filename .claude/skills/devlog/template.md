# Devlog entry template

A practical target length is **700–900 words**, which most readers can finish in about five minutes.

```text
---
date: <day as in "30th October 2026">
commit: <full hash of the latest commit of the changes, once they were finished>
---

# NNN — Title

## Hook

Present the interesting question or challenge. (About 1 minute.)

## Context

Explain where this fits into the game.

## Main Story

Choose one:

- Feature
- Problem
- Decision
- Lesson
- Experiment

Describe:

- Why
- Alternatives
- Final approach
- Tradeoffs

(About 3 minutes. A subtitle may follow the heading, e.g. `## Main Story — Making Combat Easier to Follow`, and `###`
subsections may split it.)

## Key Takeaways

Three to five concise lessons.

## What's Next

A brief preview of the next challenge.
```

This format balances storytelling, technical depth, and consistency while remaining sustainable over the lifetime of a
long-running game project.
